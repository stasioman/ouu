Smak — Food Delivery App

End-to-end design of a food delivery app: onboarding, restaurant catalogue, checkout and order tracking. 40+ screens, interactive prototype, handoff-ready component library in Figma.

Client
Smak
Role
UX/UI Designer
Year
2025
Focus
UX Research · UI Kit

The brief

Smak needed a first version of their delivery app that could go from search to a placed order in under a minute. The existing prototype was a desktop flow squeezed into a phone, and testers kept losing track of where they were.

What I did

  • Research — 8 interviews with regular delivery users, plus a competitor benchmark of 6 apps. The pattern was always the same: people decide on a restaurant first, dishes second.
  • Flows — restructured the app around restaurant → dish → checkout instead of the category-first navigation the old build used.
  • UI — 40+ screens, including empty, loading and error states, all built from a single component library in Figma.
  • Testing — two rounds of usability testing with clickable prototypes, five participants each.

Result

Time to first order in testing dropped from 3:10 to 48 seconds, and the component library became the base for the team’s later web ordering page.

Screens

The catalogue, dish detail and order tracking screens were the three that carried most of the traffic in the prototype, so they got the most iteration.
