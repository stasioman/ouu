Mobile App · 2024

Wander — Travel Booking

Concept of a weekend-trip booking app: smart search, hotel cards with honest photos, one-tap itinerary. Focus on fast decision-making — 3 taps from search to booking.

Client
Self-initiated
Role
Product Designer
Year
2024
Focus
Concept · Prototyping

The idea

Most travel apps optimise for choice. Wander optimises for deciding: a shortlist of three stays per trip, with photos that show the room as it is, not as the brochure claims.

What I did

  • Concept — defined the product around a three-stay shortlist instead of an endless result list.
  • Visual direction — large, unstyled photography; the UI steps back and lets the room carry the decision.
  • Prototype — a clickable Figma prototype covering search, shortlist, itinerary and checkout in three taps per step.

Result

Presented as a portfolio concept and later used to pitch the shortlist pattern to two client projects.
