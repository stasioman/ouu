Nordbank — Banking Dashboard

Redesign of an online banking dashboard: balance overview, spending analytics and card management. New 120-component design system, WCAG AA contrast, dark-mode ready.

Client
Nordbank
Role
UX/UI Designer
Year
2025
Focus
Dashboard · Design System

The brief

The dashboard had grown feature by feature for six years. Every team had added their own card, and users could not answer the one question they opened it for: how much money do I have right now?

What I did

  • Information architecture — cut 31 dashboard widgets down to 9, grouped into balance, spending and cards.
  • Design system — 120 components with tokens for colour, spacing, elevation and type. Every token resolves in both light and dark mode.
  • Accessibility — all text and interactive states verified against WCAG AA; charts got pattern fills so they stay readable without colour.
  • Handoff — components documented with usage rules and do/don’t examples, so three frontend teams could build in parallel.

Result

Support tickets about “where is my balance” dropped noticeably after release, and the design system is now the default for new Nordbank products.
