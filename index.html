HOW I WORK

Design thinking.

1

EMPATHIZE

Interviews and observation to understand users, their goals and frustrations.

2

DEFINE

Turning insights into a sharp problem statement, personas and user flows.

3

IDEATE

Benchmarks, wireframes and exploration of several directions before committing.

4

PROTOTYPE

Clickable prototypes and a clean UI kit, tested with real users, iterated fast.

5

TEST

Usability testing, developer handoff and measuring what actually improved.
